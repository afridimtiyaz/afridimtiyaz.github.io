Project overview

I wrote a product help guide from scratch to explain an enterprise software migration workflow. The guide was designed to help users understand the process, follow the required steps, and find guidance for common migration issues.

Documentation challenge

A migration workflow involves more than a sequence of actions. Users need to understand prerequisites, evaluate available options, follow the process in the correct order, and know what to expect after migration.

The documentation needed to bring these topics together in a structure users could navigate and follow.

Guide structure

The guide follows the order in which users meet each question during a migration.

  1. PrerequisitesWhat users need in place before they start.
  2. Migration optionsThe available choices and what each one means for the user.
  3. Migration procedureStep-by-step instructions in the order they must be done, with screenshots at key stages.
  4. RestrictionsWhat changes or is limited once the migration is complete.
  5. Post-migration stepsWhat to expect afterward and where to go next.
  6. Troubleshooting and FAQsAnswers grouped around the questions users have during the process.

My contribution

  • Created the documentation structure from scratch.
  • Wrote prerequisite information and procedural instructions.
  • Explained migration choices and their implications.
  • Organized post-migration guidance and restrictions.
  • Structured troubleshooting content and frequently asked questions.
  • Used screenshots to illustrate key stages of the workflow.

My approach

  1. Establish the contextExplain the purpose of the migration and the information users need before starting.
  2. Organize the procedurePresent the workflow in a logical sequence, separating choices and their consequences.
  3. Explain the outcomeDescribe the post-migration experience and direct users to the appropriate next steps.
  4. Support recoveryGroup troubleshooting guidance and FAQs around questions users may have during the process.
