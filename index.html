    How I work

    The same five steps sit behind every guide, help page, and UI string I write.

    1. UnderstandLearn the feature hands-on and clarify requirements with developers, product managers, and SMEs.
    2. StructureDecide what the reader needs first and organize topics around their tasks.
    3. WriteUse plain language, consistent terms, and steps that can be followed without guessing.
    4. ValidateReproduce every workflow in the product and resolve discrepancies with engineering and QA.
    5. MaintainUpdate content with each release and fix gaps that support tickets reveal.
